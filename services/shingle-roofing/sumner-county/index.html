Shingle Roofing for Sumner County homeowners

Sumner County stretches from lake-adjacent neighborhoods in Hendersonville and Gallatin to farmland around Portland and Westmoreland, so one week we are on a brick two-story and the next on a rural home with outbuildings.

Sumner County homes under mature trees, especially near the lakes and in older Hendersonville and Gallatin neighborhoods, take a beating from debris and storms. A fresh shingle roof with proper underlayment and ventilation protects the home and keeps the exterior looking sharp for years.

How we install a shingle roof

  • Full tear-off of the old roofing down to the decking.
  • Inspect and repair any damaged decking before anything new goes on.
  • Install underlayment, drip edge, flashing and proper ventilation, not just shingles.
  • Nail every shingle to manufacturer specs so the roof performs for years.
  • Magnet-sweep the yard and haul away all debris. Most roofs are finished in a single day.
