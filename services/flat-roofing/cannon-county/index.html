Flat & Low-Slope Roofing for Cannon County homeowners

Cannon County is quiet farm country around Woodbury and Auburntown. Homes, barns and outbuildings sit on open acreage, so durability and low maintenance matter more than anything.

For Cannon County homes and small commercial buildings with a flat or low-slope section, we provide repair and replacement with a straightforward quote. Seams and flashing are where most leaks begin, so that is where we focus.

What matters on a low-slope roof

  • Drainage: water has to leave the roof quickly, or it will find a way in.
  • Seams and flashing: these are where nearly all flat roof leaks start.
  • The right membrane for the building, quoted clearly before work begins.
