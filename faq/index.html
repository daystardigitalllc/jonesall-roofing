FAQ

Roofing questions, answered

Straight answers about estimates, timelines, repairs and replacements from your Mt. Juliet roofer.

Get a Free Estimate(615) 485-0213
Do you offer free roofing estimates?

Yes. Call (615) 485-0213 or send a request through our contact form and we will schedule a time to look at your roof, then give you a clear written quote with no pressure.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in Mt. Juliet and serve Wilson County plus the seven counties that touch it: Davidson, Rutherford, Sumner, Smith, Trousdale, DeKalb and Cannon.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most homes are finished in a single day, weather permitting. Larger or more complex roofs can take longer, and we will tell you up front.

Will you clean up after the job?

Yes. Customers consistently mention that we leave no debris behind. We haul away the old roofing and sweep the yard before we leave.

How do I know if I need a repair or a full replacement?

If damage is limited and the rest of the roof is in good shape, a repair may be all you need. If the roof is old, worn out or failing in several places, replacement is usually the smarter investment. We will give you an honest recommendation either way.

Do I have to pay up front?

Customers tell us they paid only once they were completely satisfied with the finished roof. Ask us about payment terms when you request your quote so everything is in writing.

Will you try to upsell me on extras?

No. Our quotes are straight and the price you are quoted is the price you pay, with no hidden fees and no pressure to add extras you do not need.

Can you install gutters with my new roof?

Yes. Many customers have new gutters and downspouts installed together with their roof so the whole exterior is done at once.

Can you help with an insurance claim?

We can provide the documentation and details you need for a claim. Call us to talk through your situation.

Do you do work other than roofing?

Yes. Jonesall Inc. Roofing & Construction also handles additions, remodels and exterior construction. Roofing is our main focus.

Are you local?

Yes. We are based at 2001 Lynnhaven Ct in Mt. Juliet, Tennessee, and have deep ties to the community.

Still have a question? Contact us or call (615) 485-0213.
